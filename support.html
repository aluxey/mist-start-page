Mist Support

Need help, found a bug, or have an idea for Mist — Minimal New Tab? You can contact the developer using either option below.

From the extension

Open Mist, select the feedback icon in the top-right corner, then choose Report a bug or Suggest a feature. Nothing is sent until you submit the form.

By email

Email aurelien.luxeyy@gmail.com. Please include the Mist version, your Chrome version, and the steps that reproduce the issue when possible.

Contact Mist support

Privacy and deletion requests

See the privacy policy for information about data handling, retention, and deletion requests. Do not include passwords or other sensitive information in a support message.
